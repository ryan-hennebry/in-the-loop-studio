# The plan

- Scope: `src/pages/plan.astro`, its three diagram components and its use of the shared layout.
- Mode: read.
- Audience: founders and early operators who want to understand how In The Loop intends to learn what an agent-native startup should become.
- Job: explain why the company exists, how Signal, Skills, Harness, Agents and Newsletter connect, and how learning from use improves the system.
- Primary action: understand the plan; the linked identity returns home.
- Proof and content: only the exact supplied Plan copy and the three stated system relationships.
- Constraints: established A6, Inter, white-paper and hairline system; no invented claims; static accessible SVG; distinct desktop and mobile diagram geometry; functional diagram grey `#858B90`; narrow visible labels at least 12.25px; no decorative motion.

## Chosen direction

A continuous-system essay. The argument begins with uncertainty, exposes the complete five-part system, then moves through Signal, Skills, Harness, Agents and Newsletter. Each diagram arrives immediately after the relationship it explains. Seed `13e54a9e`, assigned grounded structure five.
